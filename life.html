Tennis

Since August 2025 I have played USTA league tennis with Nash Smash, a team a Lehigh professor started on something of a whim. We play in the 6.0 mixed doubles and 6.0 men's doubles leagues. As the name suggests, our jerseys carry a Nash equilibrium worked out on a tennis court.

Why the name fits

A serve is a small game. If a server always goes wide, the returner learns to lean that way, so the best a server can do is mix directions in a way that leaves the returner nothing to gain from guessing. Walker and Wooders (2001, American Economic Review) checked this against serves in Wimbledon finals and found that top players come close to equilibrium play.
