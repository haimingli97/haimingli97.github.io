Tennis

Nash Smash: Coordination at the Net

Abstract

I study a game played by two doubles partners. Each player decides, at the same time, whether to come to the net or stay at the baseline. Coming in pays off only if your partner comes in too. The game has three Nash equilibria, and my team wears all of them.

Since August 2025 I have played USTA league tennis with Nash Smash, a team started by a Lehigh professor. We play in the 6.0 mixed doubles and 6.0 men's doubles leagues. The front of our jersey prints the game, and the back prints its solution (Figure 1).

Both partners at the net is the best outcome for the team. Both staying back is safe but worth less. The trouble comes when only one of you goes in. So there are two pure equilibria, both at the net or both at the baseline, and a mixed one in which each player goes in half the time. Our working hypothesis is that good partners coordinate on (1, 1). Data collection is ongoing.
