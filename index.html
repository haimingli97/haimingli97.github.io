I am a health economist and a Ph.D. candidate at Lehigh University, where I expect to complete my degree in May 2027. My research asks how state and local health policies spill across the borders that define them — who changes behavior when a law stops at a state line, and who bears the cost on the other side.

My job market paper studies the mental health consequences of recreational cannabis legalization in New Jersey for residents of neighboring Pennsylvania. Related work examines psychiatric hospital closures, dental coverage and cardiovascular spending, and the reach of fentanyl control policy.

I am on the job market in the 2026–27 academic year.
