I am a Ph.D. candidate in Business and Economics at Lehigh University and will complete my degree in May 2027. I study health policy, with a focus on substance use and mental health, using quasi-experimental methods and administrative data.

My papers ask how cannabis legalization in one state affects mental health care in its neighbor, whether closing state psychiatric hospitals shifts care onto acute care hospitals, and whether dental coverage lowers the cost of heart disease. I will present my job market paper at APPAM and the Southern Economic Association in November 2026.

As an Adjunct Professor at Lehigh, I taught Intermediate Microeconomic Analysis in Summer 2026 and will teach Econometric Software in Spring 2027.

Job Market Paper

Cross-Border Spillovers of Recreational Cannabis Legalization on Mental Health: Evidence from Pennsylvania

After New Jersey legalized recreational cannabis in 2021, psychiatric hospitalizations in Pennsylvania ZIP codes within two hours of its dispensaries rose by about 7 percent compared with matched ZIP codes farther away. Evaluations of legalization that stop at the state line miss this cost.
